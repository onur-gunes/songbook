# Demo book — public-domain sources

Every song in the bundled `demo` book is public domain in both the United
States and the European Union (author's death + 70 years). The chord charts are
new, simple arrangements written for this project; no copyrighted transcription
or arrangement was copied.

## Notes

- **Chord progressions are not copyrightable.** The charts here are original
  arrangements; they do not reproduce any publisher's transcription, tab or
  annotation.
- **Beware modern versions.** "House of the Rising Sun" (The Animals) and
  "Scarborough Fair" (Simon & Garfunkel) have copyrighted arrangements. Only
  the traditional songs are used here, with fresh chord charts.
- **Translations and new arrangements** of a public-domain song can carry their
  own copyright. Nothing of that kind is included.
- **Sound recordings** have separate copyrights. The app ships no audio, only
  text and chords.
